n<5 suppressed: any value with fewer than 5 events over the selected range is hidden and counted as suppressed.

Accepted biases: opt-out, blocked-network and corporate-proxy users are undercounted; custom formatters and loaders are masked as "custom"; firstRun is approximate (especially in containers); fake events are only mitigated by the anti-abuse layers. Events are an upper bound, distinct configs a lower bound; the true number lies in between. Local and CI units differ (one event per distinct config per machine vs one per CI run); "all" adds them together. Totals of debounced measures are sums of daily values.
