Usage patterns

Reading this page

n<5 suppressed: any value with fewer than 5 events over the last 90 days is hidden and counted as suppressed. The rule applies to the 90-day total of each value; daily figures of a shown value are not suppressed individually.

What is counted

Anonymous and aggregated data from the last 90 days. Local and CI runs are counted together; the CI vs local chart and the CI provider panel split them.

Accepted biases

Opt-out, blocked-network and corporate-proxy users are undercounted. Custom formatters and loaders are masked as "custom".
